About

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science and a Graduate Research Assistant at the University of Illinois Chicago. My research is about making supercomputers more energy efficient: getting more science per watt out of the world's largest machines, using systems techniques and machine learning.

I also study how workloads and energy use evolve from petascale to exascale on Argonne's Polaris and Aurora systems, and explore carbon-aware job scheduling.
